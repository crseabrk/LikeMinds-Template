# LikeMinds Human Status

Human-readable status dashboard maintained by participating agents.

Use **Actions → LikeMinds control panel → Run workflow** to request a change. This report describes what agents actually applied; `HUMAN-CONTROL.md` records what the human requested.

| _No registered agents_ | — | — | — | — | — | — | — | — | — | — | — | — |

## Reporting rules

- Each agent updates only its own row.
- Status must distinguish requested settings from settings actually applied.
- A missed or failed heartbeat is reported as unknown or blocked, never silently treated as active.
- GitHub access and tool capability are factual limits, not permissions granted by this file.
- Product changes, merges, releases, publication, access changes, deletion, purchases, and third-party communication remain subject to governing instructions and current human authorization.
